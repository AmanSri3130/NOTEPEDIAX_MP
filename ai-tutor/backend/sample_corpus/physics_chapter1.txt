# Motion in a Straight Line

Kinematics is the branch of mechanics that describes the motion of points, bodies, and systems of bodies without considering the forces that cause them to move.

## Velocity and Acceleration

Velocity is the rate of change of position with respect to time, whereas acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

$$ v = \frac{dx}{dt} $$

$$ a = \frac{dv}{dt} $$

## Example Problem
Example: A car travels at a constant velocity of 20 m/s for 10 seconds. What is the total distance covered?
Solution:
Given v = 20 m/s, t = 10 s.
Distance d = v * t
d = 20 * 10 = 200 meters.
